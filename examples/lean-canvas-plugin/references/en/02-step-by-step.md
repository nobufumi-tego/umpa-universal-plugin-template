# Lean Canvas - Step-by-Step Implementation

## Prerequisites

- Paper or whiteboard
- Team members (optional)
- Customer interview data

## Process

### Step 1: Define Problems

Identify up to 3 customer pain points based on interviews.

**DO**: Use actual customer feedback  
**DON'T**: Guess what customers need

### Step 2: Identify Customer Segments

Describe specific customer profiles in detail.

Example: "Freelance developers with 3+ ongoing projects"

### Step 3: Create Your Solution

Define minimal features that solve the problem (MVP focus).

**Key**: Don't include all features. Keep it minimal.

### Step 4: Craft Your Value Proposition

Write one sentence explaining why you're different from competitors.

Template: "For [customer], [solution] delivers [benefit]"

### Step 5: Identify Channels

How will you reach customers?
- Web, mobile app
- Social media, partnerships
- Direct sales

### Step 6: Define Revenue Model

How will you make money?
- Subscription
- One-time purchase
- Freemium
- B2B licensing

### Step 7: List Cost Structure

Major expenses:
- Personnel, infrastructure, marketing

### Step 8: Define Key Metrics

How will you measure success?
- MAU (Monthly Active Users)
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- LTV (Lifetime Value)

### Step 9: Identify Unfair Advantage

What can't competitors copy?
- Founder expertise
- Proprietary data
- Partnerships

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Vague Problems

**Solution**: Interview 5+ customers to identify real pain points

### Mistake 2: Target Too Broad

**Solution**: Start with one specific customer segment

### Mistake 3: Too Many Features

**Solution**: Focus on MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

## Checklist

- [ ] Problems are based on actual customer interviews
- [ ] Customer segments are clearly defined
- [ ] Solution is minimal (MVP)
- [ ] Differentiation from competitors is clear
- [ ] Revenue model is realistic
- [ ] Team agrees on all elements
