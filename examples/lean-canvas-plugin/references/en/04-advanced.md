# Advanced Techniques & Deep Dive

## Advanced Techniques

### Technique 1: Pivot Strategy

Systematically change business direction based on customer feedback.

#### When to Pivot

- Customer feedback differs from assumptions
- New competitors emerge
- Technical constraints prevent original solution

#### Types of Pivots

1. **Customer Pivot**: Same solution, different target segment
2. **Feature Pivot**: Same segment, focus on different feature
3. **Platform Pivot**: Major business model change

#### Real Example: Slack

- **Original**: Internal communication tool for game developers
- **Pivot**: Team chat platform for all businesses
- **Result**: IPO at $27B+ valuation

## FAQ

**Q: Lean Canvas vs Business Plan?**

A: 
- Lean Canvas: Hypothesis-based, flexible, quick to create
- Business Plan: Detailed, validated, for investors/loans

**Q: How detailed should it be?**

A: First draft: "Good enough to start." Detail comes later.

**Q: Can I do this alone?**

A: Yes. But team alignment is valuable for consensus.
