## FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between Lean Canvas and Business Model Canvas?**  
A: Lean Canvas is simplified for startups, while Business Model Canvas is more detailed and suitable for larger organizations.

**Q: How often should I update my Lean Canvas?**  
A: Every 3 months or when major business changes occur.
