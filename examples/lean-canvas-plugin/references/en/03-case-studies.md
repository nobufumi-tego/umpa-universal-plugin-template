# Case Studies: Learning from Real Examples

## Success Cases

### Wantedly - B2B Recruitment Platform

**Problem**: Job listings don't convey company culture

**Customer**: Engineers, designers, specialized professionals

**Solution**: Company stories and employee interviews in job listings

**Results**: 1M+ users, grew to IPO

**Learning**: Focus on what customers truly need, not surface-level features

---

### Sansan - Business Card Management SaaS

**Problem**: Sales teams spend too much time managing client information

**Solution**: Scan business cards with phone, auto-digitized to cloud

**Revenue Model**: B2B SaaS (monthly subscription)

**Results**: Grew into a unicorn, went public

**Learning**: Simple features solving big pain points win

---

## Failure Cases

### Case Study: Handwriting Digitization App

**Problem Flaw**: "Many people must need this" (guessed, not validated)

**Implementation**: Built handwriting-to-digital app over 3 months

**Failure**: No users after launch

**Cause**: No customer interviews done before building

**Lesson**: **Validate assumptions with customers before development**

---

## Success Patterns

1. **Customer-Centric**: Lean Canvas based on real interviews
2. **Speed**: Launch fast, iterate often
3. **Continuous Improvement**: Review every 3 months

## Failure Patterns

1. **Assumption-Based**: Filled in canvas without customer input
2. **Feature-Focused**: Built what team wanted, not what customers needed
3. **Static Document**: Created once, then ignored
