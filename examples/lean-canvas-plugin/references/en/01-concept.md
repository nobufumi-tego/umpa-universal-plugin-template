# Lean Canvas - Background & Theory

## What is Lean Canvas?

Lean Canvas is a one-page business model template optimized for startups and early-stage businesses.

Created in 2011 by Eric Ries based on the "Lean Startup" methodology.

## Why Is It Important?

### Traditional Business Plan vs Lean Canvas

| Aspect | Business Plan | Lean Canvas |
|--------|---|---|
| Length | 20-50 pages | 1 page |
| Time to Create | Months | Hours |
| Target | Large corporations | Startups |
| Flexibility | Low | High |

### Business Benefits

- **Efficiency**: Define key elements in just hours
- **Clarity**: 9 elements capture the entire business model
- **Executability**: Test hypotheses and improve iteratively
- **Communication**: One page makes it easy to share across teams

## The 9 Elements

1. **Problem** - Customer pain points (max 3)
2. **Customer Segments** - Target customer profiles
3. **Unique Value Proposition** - Competitive advantage
4. **Solution** - How you solve the problem
5. **Channels** - How you reach customers
6. **Revenue Streams** - How you make money
7. **Cost Structure** - Major costs
8. **Key Metrics** - Measures of success
9. **Unfair Advantage** - What competitors can't replicate

## History

### Origins (2011)
- Derived from "Lean Startup" methodology
- Simplified Business Model Canvas for startups

### Evolution
- Early: Investor pitch tool
- Today: Business planning and product development guide

## Real-World Success Cases

### Mercari (Japan's Unicorn)
- **Problem**: Inconvenient way to sell used items
- **Solution**: Mobile marketplace app
- **Result**: Japan's first unicorn

### SmartNews
- **Problem**: Too much information, hard to find relevant news
- **Solution**: AI-powered news curation app
- **Result**: Millions of users worldwide

## FAQ

**Q: When should I use Lean Canvas?**  
A: From idea stage through execution. Especially critical in early stages.

**Q: Do I only create it once?**  
A: No. Review and improve every 3 months.
